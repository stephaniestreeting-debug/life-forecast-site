Privacy Policy

Effective 11 September 2026

Life Forecast is a calendar app built to show you where your day or week has room — not to harvest your data. This page explains, plainly, what we collect, why, and what we deliberately don't collect.

The short version: we collect what's needed to run your account, sync your entries across devices if you choose to sign in, and process payment. We don't sell data, we don't run advertising trackers, and anything you write in The Clearing is never saved anywhere, by anyone — not even us.

Who we are

Life Forecast is operated by an independent Australian developer. You can reach us any time at hello.lifeforecast@gmail.com.

What we collect

What we hold about you depends on how you use the app.

  • If you never create an account: your entries and preferences live only on your device. We don't receive or store them.
  • If you sign in (optional): we store your email address, your trial/purchase status, and — if you use sync — your forecast entries, categories, preferences, and check-ins, so they're available across your devices. This is handled through our backend provider, Supabase.
  • Payment: when you pay the one-time A$9.99 unlock fee, your payment is processed directly by Stripe. We never see or store your full card details — we receive only confirmation that payment succeeded and a transaction reference.
  • Support emails: if you email us, we receive whatever you send, so we can help. Support email arrives at a dedicated inbox and isn't used for anything beyond responding to you.
  • Standard technical logs: like almost every website and app, our hosting provider (Vercel) automatically logs basic technical information (such as IP address, browser type, and request timestamps) for security and reliability. We don't use this for tracking or advertising.

The Clearing is the one exception to everything above. The Clearing is a private space in the app with no save button on purpose. Nothing you type there is stored, synced, backed up, or transmitted anywhere — not to our servers, not to Supabase, not to us. It exists only on your screen, in the moment, and disappears when you leave it. We've built an automated safeguard into the app itself that blocks any change from ever connecting Clearing content to our backend.

Crisis-language support

If something you type suggests you may be in distress, the app can show you a support card with real regional helplines. This detection happens entirely on your own device, in memory, in real time — it is never stored, logged, or sent to us or anyone else. We don't know if this feature has ever been shown to you, and we have no way of finding out. It exists solely to put help in front of you in the moment, nothing more.

How we use what we collect

  • To run your account and remember your trial or purchase status
  • To sync your entries and preferences across your devices, if you've signed in and enabled sync
  • To process your one-time payment securely
  • To respond when you contact us for support
  • To keep the app secure and working reliably

We do not use your data for advertising, and we do not sell, rent, or trade your data to anyone.

Who we share data with

We use a small number of trusted service providers to run Life Forecast. Each only receives what it needs to do its job:

  • Supabase — hosts our database and authentication, with access controls that keep every user's data isolated from every other user's.
  • Stripe — processes payments. Stripe has its own privacy policy governing the payment details you give it directly.
  • Vercel — hosts the app and website.
  • Google / ImprovMX — route email sent to our support address into our inbox.

We don't use third-party advertising or analytics trackers on the app or website. Any cookies present are limited to what's essential for sign-in and checkout to function.

How long we keep it, and how to delete it

We keep your account data for as long as your account exists. If you'd like your account and data deleted, email us at hello.lifeforecast@gmail.com and we'll remove it. Because Life Forecast is currently a small, independently-run app, deletion is handled manually by us rather than through an automated self-service tool — we aim to action requests promptly.

Payments and refunds

Every purchase is preceded by a full 7-day free trial, so you can try the app before paying. Because of that, refunds are offered for genuine technical problems within 14 days of purchase, rather than a simple change of mind. If you purchase through an app store in future, refunds for that purchase are handled by Apple or Google directly, under their own policies.

Children's privacy

Life Forecast isn't directed at children, and we don't knowingly collect personal information from children under 16. If you believe a child has provided us with personal information, please contact us and we'll delete it.

Where your data is processed

Life Forecast is built and operated in Australia, and our service providers (including Supabase, Stripe, and Vercel) may process and store data on servers located outside Australia. Each of these providers maintains its own security and privacy safeguards.

Your rights

Wherever you're located, you can ask us at any time to access, correct, or delete the personal information we hold about you, or to export a copy of your synced entries. Australian users have rights under the Privacy Act 1988 (Cth); users elsewhere may have additional rights under their local law (such as the GDPR in the UK/EU). To exercise any of these, just email us — hello.lifeforecast@gmail.com.

Security

We take reasonable steps to protect your data, including database-level access rules that keep each user's data separate from every other user's, and secure, industry-standard payment processing through Stripe. No system is perfectly secure, but we treat this carefully because it's your data, not ours.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes in a meaningful way, we'll update the date at the top of this page. We'll always aim to make Life Forecast's approach to your data easy to understand, not just easy to defend.

Contact us

Questions, requests, or concerns about your data are always welcome at hello.lifeforecast@gmail.com.
