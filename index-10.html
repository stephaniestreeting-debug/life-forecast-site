From the founder

Why I Built Life Forecast

I wanted a calendar that felt like it had my back, not one that just kept score.

My calendar could tell me exactly what I had to do, but never where I actually had time free — for friends, for rest, or for the things that mattered beyond what was already booked.

Nothing out there was built around that question, so I decided to build it myself.

Life Forecast helps you see not just what fills your time, but where you have room for what matters. When life feels overwhelming, Breathing Room offers short, guided exercises to help you pause and reset, while The Clearing gives you somewhere to put your thoughts down and let them go — without saving or keeping them.

I built Life Forecast because I wanted to understand the load I was carrying, see where I had room to cope with it, and find ways to take some of that time back for myself.

Common questions

Frequently asked questions

What is Life Forecast?

Life Forecast is a calendar that shows you where your week actually has room — for rest, for people you care about, for what matters. It's not a to-do list, and it isn't built around what's overdue.

How much does it cost?

It's free to try for 7 days, with full access to every feature. After that, a one-time payment of A$9.99 unlocks it permanently. There's no subscription — nothing renews or charges again.

Do I need an account?

No, an account is optional. Signing in lets your forecast sync across every device you use. If you'd rather not sign in, everything stays saved on the device you're using, as long as you don't clear your browser data or delete the app.

What is The Clearing?

The Clearing is a space to write something down and let it go. Nothing you write there is saved, read, or stored — by anyone, including us.

What is Breathing Room?

Breathing Room is a set of short, guided exercises you can use when a day feels like a lot. They're there to help you pause and reset — not to lecture you.

Is my data private and secure?

Yes. Your schedule and entries are stored securely and privately. What syncs and where depends on whether you're signed in (see "Do I need an account?" above). The Clearing is never saved or stored under any circumstances, by anyone, including us.

What if I don't like it after paying?

You already get 7 days to try Life Forecast free before paying anything. If something's genuinely not working as described after you've purchased, email us within 14 days and we'll make it right. Purchases made through the App Store or Google Play are refunded by Apple or Google directly, under their own policies.

Is it available on iPhone/Android?

Life Forecast works right now in any browser, on any device — no app store needed. Dedicated iOS and Android app store versions are coming soon.

What happens if I write something concerning?

If something you write ever suggests you're going through something serious, Life Forecast quietly offers real local support resources. This is never logged, tracked, or judged.

Can I use it on more than one device?

Yes. If you're signed in, your forecast and your purchase both follow you to any device you use.
