Why time management feels broken

You are not a machine. Your calendar shouldn't treat you like one.

Most calendar apps were built for corporate managers who want to pack eight meetings into an eight-hour day. They measure clock time, not human energy.

A 30-minute doctor's appointment takes the same calendar space as a 30-minute coffee with an old friend. But emotionally and cognitively, they aren't the same at all. Life Forecast is built around cognitive demand: how much of you an event actually asks for.

Questions & answers

Everything you might want to know.

How does the 7-day free trial work?
You can open the app and use every single feature completely free for 7 days. We do not ask for a credit card, an email, or a sign-up. It just works on your device right away.
How much does it cost after the trial?
A single one-time payment of A$9.99 unlocks the entire app forever. There are zero subscriptions, no monthly charges, and no hidden fees. If you prefer to unlock right away without waiting, you can do that too.
Is my schedule data private?
Completely private. Life Forecast stores your calendar entries directly in your browser's local storage on your own device. The Clearing (our overwhelm tool) is zero-storage: words you type there are erased the moment you close it and are never saved anywhere.
Can I install it like an app on my phone?
Yes! On your iPhone or iPad, tap the Share button in Safari and choose "Add to Home Screen". On Android, tap the three dots in Chrome and tap "Install app". It will sit on your home screen with its own calm icon, just like any native app.
Can I sync between my phone and computer?
Yes. If you create an optional account, you can sync your schedule safely across multiple devices so your phone and computer stay in harmony.
